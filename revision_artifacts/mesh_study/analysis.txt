CAVEAT THE MANUSCRIPT MUST STATE: the two runs used DIFFERENT baselines
  neural SDF   J0=37.1499  Jfinal=30.9404  reduction=16.71%  FlexiCubes res=48
  FFD 7x7x7    J0=36.8845  Jfinal=31.0976  reduction=15.69%  FlexiCubes res=64
  FFD 5x5x5    J0=36.8845  Jfinal=31.6468  reduction=14.20%  FlexiCubes res=64
  The neural run starts from the RECONSTRUCTED surface (J0=37.1499); the FFD
  runs start from the CAD surface (J0=36.8845), 0.72% lower. The reported
  reductions are therefore relative to different starting points, and part of
  the 16.71 vs 15.69 percentage-point gap is that offset rather than optimizer
  performance. The defensible comparison is the absolute final drag above.

ADJOINT CONVERGENCE: in all 9 runs the primal converged (606-924 iterations)
  while the adjoint reached its 1000-iteration cap without meeting its 1e-5
  residualControl. This affects the sensitivity field (the descent direction),
  not the drag values tabulated here, which come from the converged primal.
